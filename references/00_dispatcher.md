# SOP: PLR-to-Sales Copy & Offer Conversion Engine

> Standard Operating Procedure for repurposing informative Private Label Rights (PLR) content into high-converting sales letters, Video Sales Letter (VSL) scripts, and landing pages across 8 commercial business models.

**Related:** If the source is still raw PLR, run `kirby-plr-personalizer` first. The 17-step Direct Response letter lives in `kirby-direct-response-copywriting` — this skill picks the offer model and maps PLR into that letter. Load `audience_profile.yaml`, `voice_dna.yaml`, and `storyline_bank.json`. Do not steal the `sales-copy` or `vsl` triggers.

---

## 1. The 8 Commercial Offer Models

Select the appropriate model for the target commercial asset:

### 1. Self-Paced Digital Course / Video Training
* **Core Hook:** Translates PLR steps into modular "phases" or "sprints." Emphasizes independence and lifetime access.
* **Key Objection:** *"Will I actually finish this?"* -> Mitigate with step-by-step checklists and micro-action items.

### 2. Service-Based Business Landing Page
* **Core Hook:** Uses the PLR to diagnose the client's complex problem, then positions the provider as the specialized expert who handles it for them.
* **Key Objection:** *"Can't I just do this myself?"* -> Demonstrate the costly pitfalls of DIY trial-and-error.

### 3. High-Ticket Coaching / Advisory
* **Core Hook:** Positions the PLR framework as the proprietary curriculum of a high-touch mentorship or mastermind.
* **Key Objection:** *"Is this generic advice?"* -> Focus on personalized implementation, accountability, and custom feedback loops.

### 4. Physical Product / E-Commerce Complement
* **Core Hook:** Positions the PLR guide as the critical companion guide that guarantees success with a physical tool or supplement.
* **Key Objection:** *"Why do I need information with my physical product?"* -> Frame as maximizing product ROI.

### 5. Recurring Membership / Private Community
* **Core Hook:** Uses PLR to establish the ongoing need for continuous updates, peer accountability, and monthly tactical drops.
* **Key Objection:** *"Why stay subscribed?"* -> Frame as an evolving ecosystem, not a one-and-done library.

### 6. Software / SaaS / Automation Tool
* **Core Hook:** Uses PLR to expose the manual, agonizing labor required by conventional methods, positioning the software as the 1-click automation.
* **Key Objection:** *"Can't I do this with a spreadsheet?"* -> Highlight time cost and human error margins.

### 7. Done-For-You (DFY) Agency Offer
* **Core Hook:** Complete handover. The PLR validates the strategy; the agency executes 100% of the build.
* **Key Objection:** *"How much of my time will this take?"* -> Emphasize "Zero-Lift" client onboarding.

### 8. Low-Ticket "Tripwire" / Impulse Digital Product
* **Core Hook:** Hyper-specific problem-solver at an irresistible price ($7-$27).
* **Key Objection:** *"Is this cheap for a reason?"* -> Frame as a no-brainer sample of your flagship ecosystem.

## 2. The 10-Part Sales Page Blueprint

Every sales letter generated by this skill enforces this sequence (retaining linear long-form flow for VSLs and sales letters in Models 1, 3, 5, 8; for **landing-page offer models 2, 4, 6, 7**, render the **Step 0 Above-the-Fold Block** immediately in the initial viewport rather than burying CTA and proof beneath a 5-section agitation/epiphany wall):

0. **Above-the-Fold Block (Landing-Page Models 2, 4, 6, 7 Only):** Surface the primary CTA (booking, calculator, `tel:`, or price anchor) and verified review proof in the initial viewport (per `kirby-text-first-cro` §1.9), then transition into Steps 1–10.
1. **Pre-Head & Primary Headline:** Call out the specific avatar and state the primary quantifiable promise or contrarian revelation.
2. **The Problem / Agitation:** Detail the visceral symptoms of the reader's current pain point.
3. **The False Solutions (Fair Framing):** Concede where legitimate alternatives or competitors genuinely work well, then isolate the exact structural gap where they break down for this avatar (no strawman dismissals).
4. **The Epiphany / Turning Point:** Introducing the root cause (The Hidden Barrier).
5. **The Proprietary Mechanism:** Giving the PLR method a unique, branded name (e.g., *"The Delta-Pacing Matrix"*).
6. **The Core Offer Reveal:** Product mockup description and breakdown of modules/components.
7. **Social Proof & Lived Experience (Zero-Invention):** Verified case studies, `storyline_bank.json` entries, or pilot results only—never fabricate metrics, dates, or testimonials. Present aggregated third-party reviews as plain-text verified review consensus with outbound source links (**NO `AggregateRating` JSON-LD** on aggregated third-party reviews).
8. **Bonuses & Risk Reversal:** 2-3 value-stack bonuses plus an **explicit guarantee with honest limits** (state exact refund window, scope conditions, and exclusions rather than a blanket "unconditional guarantee").
9. **Urgency / Call to Action:** Why waiting costs more than acting now.
10. **P.S. Summary:** Re-stating the core transformation and guarantee limits.

## 3. PLR to Direct Response Sales Page Execution

Execute the conversion using the following directives:

**Inputs Required:**
1. **PERSONALIZED PLR CONTENT:** {{INSERT_PERSONALIZED_PLR_TEXT}} (if still raw PLR, stop and run `kirby-plr-personalizer`)
2. **AUDIENCE:** `audience_profile.yaml`
3. **VOICE:** `voice_dna.yaml`
4. **STORIES:** `storyline_bank.json`
5. **PRODUCT NAME:** {{PRODUCT_NAME}}
6. **PRICE POINT:** {{PRICE_POINT}}
7. **OFFER MODEL:** {{OFFER_MODEL}} (one of the 8 models in §1)

**Copywriting Directives:**
1. Develop 3 punchy, curiosity-driven headline options with supporting subheadlines.
2. Name the core framework from the PLR with a memorable, proprietary title.
3. Never use generic sales clichés ("Act now!", "Hurry!", "Amazing offer!"). Build urgency strictly through the cost of inaction.
4. Write out the full copy for each section of the 10-Part Sales Page Blueprint.
5. Emphasize the transformation, not the file formats (sell the outcome, not the PDF).
6. **Above-the-Fold Viewport Match (Models 2, 4, 6, 7):** For landing-page offer models, place the primary CTA (booking/calculator/`tel:`/price anchor) and verified review proof in the initial viewport; retain linear long-form flow for VSL and sales-letter models (1, 3, 5, 8).
7. **Zero-Invention & Fair Framing:** Concede legitimate competitor strengths in Step 3; state explicit guarantee limits in Step 8; use only verified proof in Step 7 (plain-text review consensus + outbound links, **NO `AggregateRating` JSON-LD** on aggregated third-party reviews).
